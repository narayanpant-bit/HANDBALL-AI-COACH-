🤾 Handball AI Coach

AI-Powered Handball Training Assistant

Handball AI Coach uses computer vision and pose analysis to analyze a player's movement and provide basic training feedback.

Key Features

  • Full-body pose tracking
  • Hand and arm movement analysis
  • Joint angle calculation
  • Throwing and shooting posture detection
  • Real-time feedback

Technology

Python • OpenCV • YOLO Pose • NumPy

Project Status: Experimental MVP
